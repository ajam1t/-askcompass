The challenge

SI is only as good as the knowledge it runs on.

In most organizations, that knowledge is broken in three ways. People feel it every shift, and SI inherits the same problem.

1Static knowledgeFixed

Knowledge stuck in documents Documents become steps

Critical steps are buried in long PDFs, old attachments and wikis nobody can navigate quickly. Static content was never built to be followed, by people or by SI.

ASK COMPASS turns documents into structured procedures, with steps, decisions and the source behind each one, that people can follow and SI can reason over.

2Process driftFixed

Processes that drift One published way

When the real process lives in your best performer’s head, nothing gets updated. Quality changes with every shift, and “best practice” becomes whatever the person on shift remembers.

Each procedure has one published version. Everyone follows the same steps, and every change is drafted, reviewed, approved and recorded before it goes live.

3Disconnected systemsFixed

No single source of truth One place, linked to the work

Five tabs, three shared drives and a wiki. The right answer is in there, probably. Teams waste time searching and second-guessing, and SI has the same problem.

Procedures, policies and their sources live in one searchable place, each linked to the exact step where it applies.
