Ask Compass Studio

Change procedures safely, with a person in the loop.

Studio is where procedures are written, reviewed and kept current. Every step below works in the prototype today.

  1. Draft

    Editors change a copy. Everyone keeps following the approved version.

  2. Check

    Studio flags missing answers, dead ends and steps nobody can reach.

  3. Review

    Approvers compare old and new side by side, then approve or return it with a comment.

  4. Publish

    A new version goes live. Older versions stay in the history and can be restored.

  5. Notify and learn

    People who use the procedure are told it changed. Flags and drop-offs show what to fix next.

  • Linked procedures One procedure can hand off to another, such as an identity check, and come back.
  • Step-level search Search finds the exact step, not just the document.
  • Roles Viewer, Editor, Approver and Admin each see what they need.
  • Audit log Every edit, approval and restore is recorded.
