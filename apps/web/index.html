    Ask Compass Studio

    Change procedures safely, with a person in the loop.

    Studio is where procedures are written, reviewed and kept current. Every step below works in the prototype today.

    1. Draft

      Editors change a copy. Everyone keeps following the approved version.

    2. Check

      Studio flags missing answers, dead ends and steps nobody can reach.

    3. Review

      Approvers compare old and new side by side, then approve or return it with a comment.

    4. Publish

      A new version goes live. Older versions stay in the history and can be restored.

    5. Notify and learn

      People who use the procedure are told it changed. Flags and drop-offs show what to fix next.

    • Linked procedures One procedure can hand off to another, such as an identity check, and come back.
    • Step-level search Search finds the exact step, not just the document.
    • Roles Viewer, Editor, Approver and Admin each see what they need.
    • Audit log Every edit, approval and restore is recorded.

    How it works

    From question to completion.

    Every task follows the same three stages, whether it is a first-week onboarding question or a once-a-year approval.

    1. Ask

      Describe the task

      Ask in plain language, the way you would ask a colleague.

      We need a laptop for a contractor starting Monday

    2. Navigate

      Get a structured path

      See the applicable steps, the decisions that matter and the sources behind them.

      Check eligibilityContractor?Sponsor approval

    3. Act

      Move the work forward

      Complete each step, confirm what is required and know what remains.

    Solutions

    Designed for many kinds of work.

    Any process with steps, decisions and exceptions can become a guided path. These examples are illustrations of the approach.

    One procedure, two sides

    Clear guidance for the people doing the work, and control for the people who own it.

    For the person doing the work

    • Find answers without unnecessary searching.
    • Understand what to do next.
    • Work through unfamiliar procedures with confidence.
    • Spend less time unsure about complex tasks.
    Approved procedure

    For the people who own the process

    • Organize and govern process knowledge.
    • Maintain approved procedures in one place.
    • Communicate changes to the people they affect.
    • Improve consistency in how work is done.
    • See where guidance needs improving.

    Trust and governance

    Guidance you can check.

    People should only follow guidance when they can see where it came from. This is how Ask Compass is designed to show it.

    Source references, approved versions, owners, update dates, exceptions, change notices and human approval work in the Studio prototype today. Escalation guidance is written into individual steps.
