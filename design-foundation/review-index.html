V9 Compliance Checklist

  • INDIA ONLY — zero Nepal references in any page, SVG, testimonial, or location hierarchy
  • Design tokens from V9 section 35 — CSS custom properties throughout all pages
  • Google Fonts: Marcellus (headings) + Mukta (body/UI) + Rozha One (Devanagari) + Kalam (handwritten)
  • Color palette: Maroon #7A1220, Terracotta #C4562F, Gold #B98A2E, Ivory #FBF1DD, Leaf Green #1F5133, Indigo #2E3A6E
  • Madhubani ornamental motifs — lotus, fish, peacock, marigold toran, mandala, geometric borders
  • Hybrid hero slot — illustration fallback to coded SVG Madhubani scene
  • Search filters match V9 spec exactly: Bride/Groom, Age, Mother Tongue, Caste, Sub Caste, Self Gotra, Maternal Gotra, Mool, Education, Profession, Current Location, Job Location, Native Place, Marital Status, Lifestyle, Family Type, Verification, Marriage Timeline

  • 3D Profile Card: CSS preserve-3d flip · front (photo, match ring, chips) + back (gotra, mool, Bihar roots, all fields) · hover tilt · auto-demo · NOT Tinder-style · review-3d-profile-card.html
  • Dulhan ki Doli: cinematic storytelling scene · decorated palanquin, graceful happy bride, family blessing (aarti thali + waving child), doli bearers with walking motion, falling petals, flickering diyas · complements Varmala · "new beginning" tone · review-doli-scene.html
  • Family Connect: invite system, granular permissions, notes on profiles, privacy controls
  • Mithila Roots: Current Location, Job Location, Native District — all India-only, no exact addresses
  • Membership plans aligned to Mithila context: Parichay, Vivah, Samridh — not SaaS tier names
  • No SaaS/startup/dating-app aesthetic — warm paper textures, maroon/gold/green palette throughout
