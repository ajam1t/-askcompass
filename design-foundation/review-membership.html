Frequently Asked Questions

How does payment work? +
We accept UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and all major Indian payment methods via Razorpay. Your payment data is fully secure and never stored on our servers.
Can I cancel my membership? +
Yes, you can cancel anytime. If you cancel within 7 days of purchase, we'll refund the full amount — no questions asked.
Is my personal data safe? +
Absolutely. Contact details are only shared with profiles you've mutually connected with. Photos are locked behind the interest system. We follow all applicable Indian data protection guidelines.
What is Family Connect? +
Family Connect lets you invite family members — parents, siblings — to view and participate in the match-finding process with their own controlled access. They can view selected profiles and send notes.
