A · Design rationale

Mithila, made premium — never a SaaS template

Every decision starts from the culture and adds technology on top — not the other way around. The test: if the logo were removed, it should still unmistakably read as Mithila, then premium, then modern.

Culture-first

Madhubani line-art, handcrafted borders, warm paper. Motifs (lotus, fish, peacock, sun, diya, mandap) are structural, not stickers on a SaaS layout.

Family & trust

Warm, editorial, calm. Family Connect and verification are first-class. It should feel like a premium digital wedding invitation that happens to be software.

Deliberately not

No blue/purple SaaS gradients, no glassmorphism, no generic rounded dashboard cards, no dating-app swipe UI, no neon, no heavy dark mode.

3D profile-card concept · logged-in experience

Explore a person — never swipe them

IntroEducationCareerMithila RootsFamilyLifestylePreferencesVerification

A single elegant card the user rotates/turns to reveal faces in sequence. Deliberate Interest and Shortlist actions replace reject/accept swiping.

  • Faces: Intro → Education → Career → Roots → Family → Lifestyle → Preferences → Verification → Full profile
  • Depth via perspective + tilt; progressive information reveal
  • Full keyboard + dot navigation; a Classic View for family members
Homepage uses subtle depth/parallax only. The richer 3D belongs to the logged-in discovery experience.

F · Hero animation storyboard

A living scene, not a photo

Layer 1Madhubani frame (static)
Layer 2Mandap · sun · garlands (slow parallax)
Layer 3Couples · varmala · diyas (flame flicker)
Layer 4Falling petals (loop)
Pointer movement offsets layers by increasing depth (6→14→26→34px) for gentle depth. Touch: reduced parallax. Reduced-motion / low-power: static composition, petals off.
K · Responsive rules

Mobile is designed, not shrunk

Desktop

Two-column hero (copy left, art right), 6-up feature strip, 2-up story cards, side-by-side biodata & roots.

Mobile

Vertical hero — art moves above copy and stays prominent (not shrunk to meaninglessness). Strip 3→2 up, cards stack, flow arrows rotate, slide-in menu, tap-friendly ≥44px targets.

Breakpoints in the mockup: 900px (tablet/mobile layout) and 520px (compact). Resize homepage.html to see both compositions.
J · Animation principles

Graceful, purposeful, degradable

  • Easing: soft cultural ease cubic-bezier(.2,1,.3,1); nothing bouncy or “gamey”.
  • Draw-in for line-art (stroke-dashoffset), pop for motifs, flicker for diyas, drift for petals.
  • Parallax is subtle and pointer-driven; never moves the whole hero aggressively.
  • GPU-friendly transforms/opacity only; no layout thrash.
  • Always degradable: honours prefers-reduced-motion and low-power devices.
L · Accessibility

Decoration never blocks readability

  • Semantic landmarks, keyboard-navigable, visible focus rings
  • Decorative SVG marked aria-hidden; meaningful art has labels
  • Contrast: charcoal on paper, paper on maroon all pass AA
  • Touch targets ≥ 44px; controls have text alternatives to gestures
  • Reduced-motion variant of intro & hero
  • Screen-reader labels on the wedding artwork & icons
M · Performance

Cultural richness, still fast

  • Artwork is inline SVG (crisp, tiny, no image requests) rather than heavy photos or video.
  • Lazy-load non-critical imagery; responsive sizes; WebP/AVIF; BlurHash placeholders.
  • No Three.js on the homepage; depth is pure CSS transforms. Heavier 3D only in logged-in discovery, code-split.
  • Intro never blocks first meaningful content; reduce animation on low-power devices.
SEO

Crawlable, semantic, private-by-default

  • Semantic HTML, descriptive <title> + meta description, Open Graph, canonical URL, Organization JSON-LD.
  • Target public terms: marriage biodata, biodata format/PDF, Hindi/Maithili/Sanskrit marriage biodata, Mithila matrimonial — via a handful of quality pages, not hundreds of thin ones.
  • Never expose private matrimonial profile data to crawlers.
